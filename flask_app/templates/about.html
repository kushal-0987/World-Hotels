Our Story

From a Simple Idea to a Global Network

World Hotels began with a vision: to make luxury travel accessible to everyone while maintaining the highest standards of service and comfort.

Founded in 2010 in London, we started as a small booking platform connecting travelers with boutique hotels across the UK. Today, we've grown into a global hospitality network spanning 50 countries.
